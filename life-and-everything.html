പ്രവാസ ലോകം: A Life I Never Planned

I didn't plan this life.

Actually, I didn't even want this life in the beginning.

But sometimes life doesn't really ask what you have planned. It just puts you somewhere and says, “Okay… now figure it out.” 😁

For me, that place was പ്രവാസ ലോകം — life away from home.

12 November 2018.

That was the day I arrived.

My father was the reason I came here. And honestly, at first, I was angry with him. Really angry. I was frustrated that I had been brought into a life that wasn't part of my plan. 😠

I had my own thoughts about what my life should look like.

But my father had a different idea.

At that time, I couldn't understand it.

Then Everything Changed

About a year after I arrived, in December 2019, my father travelled back to our home country for medical treatment. ✈️

And suddenly, I was here.

Working.

Looking after my family.

Trying to manage everything from far away.

And my father was back home.

Instead of making me feel better about being here, it made me even more angry.

I remember thinking about how I ended up in this situation.

Why am I here?

Why do I have to do all this?

I didn't have the answers.

Then came 2020.

COVID happened, and the whole world changed. 🦠

I was stuck here.

My father was stuck back home.

What we thought might be temporary became two and a half years of distance.

Two and a half years. ⏳

Me here.

My father there.

And somewhere during those years, without me even realizing it, I started changing. 💭

Maybe This Wasn't Just About Me

Those two and a half years taught me something I probably wouldn't have understood otherwise.

I started realizing who I was.

Not just as a person.

But as a son.

As a member of my family.

As someone who had responsibilities.

I had started looking after my whole family from that point.

And somehow, that continued.

Until today.

Even while I'm writing this, I feel something that I didn't feel when I first came here.

I feel proud of myself. 😊

Not because I became some extraordinary person.

But because I stayed.

I worked.

I took responsibility.

I looked after my family. ❤️

And I kept going, even when this wasn't the life I had originally imagined. 💪

And today, I am also very thankful to my father. 🙏

The person I was once angry with for bringing me here...

Maybe he actually saw something in me that I couldn't see in myself.

He had a clear picture of what he wanted me to become.

I just wasn't ready to understand it then.

Maybe fathers sometimes see a road for us before we are ready to see it ourselves.

And About This Blog...

One small confession.

My writing here is not going to be in chronological order. 😂

Whatever I'm feeling, whatever memory comes to me, whatever I feel like writing...

I'll write it.

So you might read something from 2018 today, something from 2026 tomorrow, and then suddenly I might start talking about food. 🍜

That's just how my brain works. 😁

You guys may even get bored because some of these stories are probably a little outdated.

I know.

Hehehe.

But maybe that's okay.

Everything today has to be fast, new and constantly updated.

Maybe, after many years, people will actually enjoy doing things the old-fashioned way again.

I hope so.

And if you're still here reading this...

thank you for coming along on my journey. 👋
