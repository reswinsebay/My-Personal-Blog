My First Journey

September 7, 2026

Welcome to my first story on Reswin's Journey.

When I say "journey", I am not talking about where I was born, where I have travelled, or how many places I have visited. This journey is something much more personal. It is about slowly discovering who I really am in the real world.

How This Started

Honestly, this blog started ഒരു തോന്നലിന്റെ പുറത്ത്. 😁

There wasn't a big plan behind it. No carefully prepared strategy. One day, I simply felt like creating a space where I could write, share, think, laugh and maybe even look back at my own thoughts someday.

And that's how Reswin's Journey began.

Finding My Own Choices

For a long time, my life depended on other people's choices. There were decisions made around me, circumstances I had to accept, and moments when I felt like I didn't really have a choice of my own.

But somewhere along the way, something changed. I started to realise that I do have choices. I have my own thoughts, my own responsibilities and my own direction to discover.

That realisation may sound simple, but for me it means a lot. It made me look at life differently.

More Than Just Me

As I started understanding myself better, I also started thinking about the people around me.

I have a family that depends on me in different ways, and I want to do something meaningful for them. At the same time, I don't want my journey to stop with just my own life. I want to believe that I can contribute something to society as well, even if it is in small ways.

I don't have all the answers yet. Honestly, I am still figuring things out. And maybe that is exactly what makes this journey interesting.

And Then There Is Food... 😁

Of course, no journey of mine would be complete without food.

I love trying new foods and discovering new food spots. I may not eat every single food item I see, but that doesn't stop me from proudly calling myself a foodieeeee! 😂

Some food experiences will be amazing. Some might make me wonder what I was thinking when I ordered them. Either way, they are part of the journey.

Why Reswin's Journey?

This blog is going to be a collection of those moments — experiences, food, thoughts, discoveries and all the random things that make life interesting.

I don't want this to be a place where everything sounds perfect. Life isn't perfect, and neither am I. I simply want to share the journey as I see it.

Maybe something I write will make you think. Maybe something will make you smile. And maybe, somewhere between the stories, you will recognise a little bit of your own journey too.

This Is Only the Beginning

I am still discovering who I am, what I want, and what I can do for the people I care about and the world around me.

So this isn't a story about having everything figured out. It is a story about finally choosing to take the next step.

And I'm proud of myself for taking it.

Thank you for being here at the beginning. There are plenty of stories still waiting to be written.

— Reswin
